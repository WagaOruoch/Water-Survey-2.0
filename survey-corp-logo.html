📋 Usage Guidelines

Logo Concept

Abstract geometric shapes represent data points and survey responses coming together. The blue (data/trust) and green (growth/action) create dynamic energy while maintaining professional credibility.

Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of one shape around the logo. Never place other elements too close to the logo.

Minimum Size

• Full logo: 120px width minimum
• Icon only: 16px minimum
• Below these sizes, use icon only
